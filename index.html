My research studies economic behavior as grounded in cognitive and biological processes. It combines economic theory with experimental methods and evidence from neuroscience and genetics. Recent work focuses on intergenerational mobility and the role of cognitive and non-cognitive traits in shaping economic outcomes.

Research Areas

  • Decision theory and game theory
  • Behavioral and experimental economics
  • Neuroeconomics
  • Genetics and economic outcomes
